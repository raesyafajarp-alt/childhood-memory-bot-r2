## Entry (one sentence, max 280 characters)

> Dad taught me to ride a bike by letting go without telling me, and years later he admitted he'd sprinted the whole street behind me, out of breath, just in case I fell.

## Why it should score better

- **A reveal, not a postcard.** Round 1 described a scene; round 2
  hides its warmth until the last clause, when the father admits he
  was running behind all along.
- **Concrete and sensory.** A whole street, sprinting, out of breath —
  the body remembers the effort even when the child didn't see it.
- **Universal and specific at once.** Almost everyone learned to ride
  a bike; almost no one knew someone was running behind them.
